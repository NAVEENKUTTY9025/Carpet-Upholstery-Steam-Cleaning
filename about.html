Our Story

Fresh & Clean was founded in 2014 with a simple mission: to provide homeowners and businesses with a deep, hygienic clean that's safe for families, pets, and the planet.

What started as a small family-run operation has grown into a trusted name in steam cleaning. We've helped thousands of customers rediscover the freshness of their carpets, sofas, and upholstery — without harsh chemicals or long drying times.

Today, we combine advanced steam technology with a genuinely caring approach. Every job is treated with the same attention to detail we'd want in our own homes.
