Aftercare & Maintenance

Most carpets dry within 4–6 hours thanks to our advanced high-powered extraction equipment. Humidity, ventilation, and carpet thickness can affect drying time. We recommend keeping windows open and using fans to speed up the process.

We recommend professional steam cleaning every 6–12 months, depending on foot traffic, pets, and allergies. High-traffic areas and homes with pets may benefit from cleaning every 4–6 months.

Vacuum high-traffic areas at least twice a week, blot spills immediately with a clean cloth, and use doormats to reduce dirt tracking. Avoid walking on damp carpets after cleaning until fully dry.

Some stains (especially oil-based or pet stains) can wick back to the surface as the carpet dries. Gently blot with a clean white cloth and contact us — we offer a satisfaction guarantee and will re-treat the area if needed.
